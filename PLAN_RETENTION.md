# Retention plan: from ~9 minutes to 20+

Average playtime is about 9 minutes. This plan looks at the game the way a 12-year-old would and lists what
to build, in the order that should move the number most.

## Why kids leave around minute 9 (my read of the current game)

1. **The tutorial ends around minute 5-8 and then nothing tells you what to do next.** The full HUD, missions
   and vendors all appear at once, but there is no single "next goal" on screen.
2. **The best things unlock just after they leave.** The Deep Rod opens at 20 min and the good playtime gifts
   at 15/20/30 min, but the average player is gone by 9. The 10-minute gift is **1 Power spin**, which can't
   be used until depth 30, so the one reward that lands in time is useless to them.
3. **Fights are watch-only.** The team fights by itself, and the kid has nothing to press for most of a run.
   (The game is "inspired by Clicker Heroes", but there is no clicking.)
4. **The island is a shopping street, far away.** It is a 150-180 stud walk from your ship, and almost every
   stand opens a menu. There is only one real thing to play there (Crab Cove), and its cooldown is 12 minutes.
5. **The mini games are hard to find or rarely on.** Live Crates come once every 30 minutes, server-wide, so
   most players never see them. Crab Cove has no score board, no best score and no reason to replay.
6. **No social buzz.** You never hear about other players' big hatches or high scores, so there's no envy or
   "I want that too".

Check before building: the Analytics onboarding funnel and the playtime gift claims in the Creator Dashboard
should show where players really drop. If you can share those numbers, the order below can be tuned.

---

## Phase 1: quick wins (days, not weeks) — fix minutes 5-15

These are mostly number changes and small UI, and target the exact window where players leave.

1. **"Next Goal" tracker on the HUD.** One line under the missions, always showing the nearest exciting
   unlock with a progress bar or countdown: "Deep Rod in 3:12", "Floor 2: 7/10 fuses", "Boss at Depth 15",
   "New team slot: 2.1M / 2.6M". When one is reached, a splash and the next one appears.
2. **Re-time the playtime gifts to the drop-off.** Front-load them and put something exciting at 8-12 min:
   - 2 min: coins · 5 min: a spell card · **8 min: a guaranteed Rare egg** · 12 min: pearls + spell ·
     **15 min: a guaranteed Epic egg** · 20 min: Golden egg · 30 min: big pearls.
   - Replace the 10-min Power spin (useless before depth 30) with something usable at once.
   - Show the next gift's countdown on the Gift Chest and as a HUD chip ("Next gift 1:20").
3. **Deep Rod at 8 minutes instead of 20.** It is the most exciting thing in the game and almost no one
   reaches it. Keep the 10 min cooldown, so it becomes a reason to stay ("one more cast in 4:10").
4. **Fast travel to the island.** A "Sail to Harbour" button on the HUD (and a "Back to my Ship" button on the
   island), with a short whoosh. Also add a **launch cannon** on each ship's bow that fires you onto the
   island: it's fun in itself, and kids will do it on purpose.
5. **Server announcements.** A banner for everyone when someone hatches an Epic+ or a Golden/Rainbow, beats a
   boss, or sets a new Crab Cove record: "TuckerFlnk hatched a RAINBOW Kraken King!". Cheap, and it creates
   the "I want that" feeling.

## Phase 2: give the kid something to press

6. **Tap to help in fights.** While your team fights, tapping the enemy deals a small hit (a few % of team
   DPS) with a satisfying pop, and builds a **Rage meter**. A full meter unlocks a big "SUPER SPLASH" button
   (screen shake, huge damage number). Makes every fight active without breaking idle play.
7. **Combo and crit numbers.** Bigger, colourful damage popups for crits and elemental "SUPER!" hits, with a
   combo counter. (The 550x number inflation already makes these look great.)
8. **Boss drama.** Bosses already have a timer. Add a "BOSS ENRAGED!" phase at 25% HP with a red tint and
   faster music, and a slow-motion final blow.

## Phase 3: make the island a playground (and upgrade the mini games)

### Crab Cove (the existing mini game)
9. **Cooldown 12 min → 4 min**, with full rewards on the first 3 plays per hour, then reduced. Kids replay
   things they're good at.
10. **Best score + leaderboard.** Show "Best: 14" on the stand, and a server/global top-10 board next to it.
11. **Power-ups on the sand:** a speed shell, a bubble shield (blocks one snap) and a magnet (pulls nearby
    eggs). Randomly placed each round.
12. **The Crab King.** In the last 15 seconds a giant crab comes out; deliver a golden egg past it for a bonus.
13. **Tiers:** Easy / Normal / Hard (more crabs, better rewards). Hard unlocks after scoring 10 on Normal.

### Cannon Gallery
14. **On-demand Target Practice:** a 60-second solo round at the ship's cannons whenever you want, with sea
    monsters popping out of the water, moving targets and a score. Cooldown 5 min.
15. **Keep the server-wide crate rain as a scheduled event,** but every 10-15 min instead of 30, with a HUD
    countdown ("CRATE RAIN in 2:30") so everyone runs to their cannons together.

### Fishing dive
16. **Things to catch on the way down:** steer through bubbles and pearls during the dive, plus a rare golden
    fish to chase. Makes every cast a tiny game, not just a wait.

### New island activities
17. **Lighthouse Obby.** A short parkour climb up the outside of the lighthouse (moving planks, jump pads,
    swinging anchors) to a chest at the top, which pays once per 20 min. A timer and a "fastest climb today"
    board. Obbies are the #1 thing kids do in Roblox games.
18. **Treasure dig.** 5 "X marks the spot" around the island every 10 minutes. Walk to one and hold E to dig:
    coins, pearls, a spell card or (rarely) an egg. Gets kids exploring the island.
19. **Your monster follows you.** Your strongest monster (or a chosen one) walks behind you on the island. Kids
    love showing off, and it makes the island feel alive when 6 players are there.
20. **Monster Races.** Every few minutes a short race on a track round the island: pick one of your monsters,
    tap to boost, top 3 win prizes. Short, social and repeatable.

## Phase 4: reasons to come back tomorrow

21. **Daily quests** (3 a day, bigger than missions, e.g. "Score 10 in Crab Cove", "Hatch an Epic").
22. **Weekly event monster** in the Abyss Crate, with a visible "leaves in 3d 4h" timer.
23. **Login calendar** (day 7: a guaranteed Epic egg) shown the moment you join.
24. **Unfinished badges:** Depth50, Depth100 and Maw have no badge ID yet. Badges are a cheap reason to play.

## Phase 5: the long game (what Dragon Arena does that we didn't)

Added after comparing with `research/dragon-arena-notes.md`. Already in the game: the Maw villain intro
(Cinema.luau, depth 300), friends coin boost, Best Depth sign over every ship, offline earnings, codes.

25. **Tide Rebirth (built).** The Tide Whirlpool in town (inner ring). After Depth 60 (then +15 per rebirth, up
    to 10) you can start again: coins and best depth reset, everything else stays. Each Tide Mark gives +40%
    battle coins for good, pearls, and a guaranteed 2-star egg. A "!" floats over the Whirlpool when one is
    ready, the ship sign shows a star count, and everyone hears about it. The leaderboard keeps your peak depth.
26. **Staggered unlocks (built, `server/DripService.luau`).** After the tutorial, the island's activities open
    one at a time: Crab Cove at +1.5 min, Lighthouse Climb at +3.5, Treasure Digs at +5.5 (minutes of play after
    the tutorial ends). Each shows a "NEW:" splash, a hint, 5-10 pearls, and a bouncing "!" where to go.
    Trying one early tells you when it opens. Existing players get everything open. Timings are `Config.DRIP`.
27. **Daily quests (built, in `MissionService`).** The Quest Board in town (just off the lighthouse plaza) gives 3
    bigger goals a day (new set every UTC day, same set all day for a player), each paying coins + pearls, and a
    bonus chest (pearls + a spell card) for all three. A toast says when one is finished and a "!" floats over
    the board until it is claimed. The login calendar is still todo (Phase 4 #23).
28. **Maw reminders (todo).** Roar announcements at depth 100/200, a Maw line in the Index, an end-of-run card
    "Maw: 142 of 300".
29. **Experience notifications (todo).** Opt-in "notify me" prompt after the first boss win.

Rule for all new UI: it must not overlap existing HUD (put it in a menu or in the world), and it has to be
checked at phone size before it ships.

## Measure it

- Add analytics for: session length per minute bucket, gift claims by tier, mini game plays, Next Goal hits.
- Ship Phase 1 alone first and compare average playtime for a few days before building the bigger phases.

## Suggested order and rough size

| # | Item | Size | Why first |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-5 | Phase 1 quick wins | small | hits the exact minute players leave |
| 9-10 | Crab Cove cooldown + best score | small | makes the existing mini game replayable |
| 6-7 | Tap to help in fights | medium | fixes the biggest "boring" moment |
| 15, 14 | Crate rain timing, Target Practice | medium | uses code that already exists |
| 17-18 | Lighthouse Obby, Treasure dig | medium | gives the island a reason to visit |
| 19-20 | Follow pet, Monster Races | larger | social and show-off |
| 21-24 | Daily systems, badges | medium | day-2 retention |
