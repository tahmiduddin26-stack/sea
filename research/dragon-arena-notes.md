# Dragon Arena — raw play notes

Source: Roblox universe 10771238997 (place 83694443310872), by "Vegle & Data 3".
Created 2026-10-16, ~4.0M visits, ~15.4k CCU on 2026-11-21, 24k favorites, 33.4k up / 403 down.
Max 6 players per server. Genre: Simulation > Tycoon. "Inspired by Dragon City and Clicker Heroes".

## Store description (summary)
- Promo code "FreeSpin" = free Powers spin. Join group = free egg.
- Hatch eggs -> elemental dragons (Fire, Water, Rock/Earth, Ice).
- Breed dragons -> new dragons.
- Defeat mobs + bosses on WAVES to earn coins.
- 5-star unique dragons.
- Evolve your plot -> unlocks new features.
- Goal: best dragon army mixing elements, reach max wave.
- Dragons FIGHT OFFLINE.

## Tutorial flow (first 2 minutes)
1. Walk to "Spin Eggs" lever on your plot -> E "Pull".
   Pulling rolls a random egg that drops in front of the lever with price + level
   (first roll: "Blaze Egg Lvl.1", 780 coins).
   Sign next to lever: "More Breed Luck — Only R$119" (dev product).
2. "Buy your first EGG!" -> walk into egg = buy (2.5K -> 1.7K) and you CARRY it (shows in hotbar slot).
3. "Place your Egg in a NEST!" -> walk to one of several straw nests on plot edge; egg auto-places,
   timer shows (6s for first egg).
4. "Open your egg when it's READY!" -> click egg -> hatch -> "Blaze Dragon Lvl.1", 1/5 stars.
   Dragon sits on the nest. Element icon (flame) above name.
5. "Try to beat the MINI BOSS!" + red FIGHT! button top-centre.
6. FIGHT: dragons fly from nests to the round dirt arena in middle of plot, nest shows "IN FIGHT!".
   Auto-battle. Wave tracker top: "Wave N", row of dots (green = cleared), skull = mini-boss, horned skull = boss.
   Enemy name + HP bar (Slime 11/11 wave 3, Bat 12/12 wave 7).
   Coins drop from kills (+16, +20 popups), fly to HUD.
7. Lost at wave 7 with one Lv1 dragon -> "Your dragons were defeated!" + banner "You need MORE DRAGONS!".
   Coins 2.5K -> 2.4K after buy+fight. Best Wave updated to 7. Dragons return to nests.
8. Lever again (hold E ~1s; tap didn't always register) -> "Frost Egg Lvl.1" 1.9K coins. Bought -> 520 left.
   Placed in nest (E "Place" prompt at nest), short timer, open with E/click when "Ready!".
   Hatch VFX: element-themed burst (water drops raining for Tide). "Team Power: N" popup on add.
9. "Breed your 2 DRAGONS!" -> big heart "Breeding!" button bottom-centre opens breed panel:
   [Dragon A slot] + [Dragon B slot] = "Lv1 + Lv1 = Lv2", time 5s, row of possible outcomes with % (~34/33/33),
   big pink "Breed!" button. Breeding is FREE in coins.
   *** BREEDING CONSUMES BOTH PARENTS *** -> they fly into one nest with hearts, timer, then a
   silhouette reveal -> ONE Lv2 dragon. Blaze(fire) + Frost(ice) -> "Comet Dragon Lvl.2", 2 stars,
   shows BOTH element icons (hybrid). This is effectively MERGE (2 x Lv N -> 1 x Lv N+1) with a random
   species/element outcome.
10. "Buy a TIDE EGG!" -> Tide egg 300 coins (water). Hatched Tide Dragon Lv1 1-star, 125 HP.
    Comet Lv2 had 177 HP.

  More Breed Luck R$119 / Insane Breed Luck R$399 / x2 Fight Speed R$59).
- Bottom: Breeding! heart button, hotbar (items/eggs being carried, event apple).
- Top wave bar: "Wave N", restart icon, speed toggle "x1", "Retreat" button.
- Plot has a 2nd tier "FLOOR LOCKED" (padlock) with more nest slots = plot evolution.
- Some nests show red "LOCKED"-style pads = locked nest slots.

## Combat / waves
- Fully auto. Dragons stand on arena, enemies spawn one at a time, HP bars on both sides.
  Dragons have HP bar + blue ability/energy bar.
- Stage = 15 waves: waves 1-7 normal, wave 8 = mini-boss (skull), 9-14 normal, wave 15 = BOSS (horned skull).
- Boss gets a letterbox cinematic intro: "BOSS: Goblin King" (green goblin w/ crown + mace).
  Boss has HP (250) and a COUNTDOWN timer bar (~20s) - must kill before timer ends.
- After stage, wave 16+ uses next stage: enemy HP jumps (Slime 13 HP @ wave 12 -> 81 HP @ wave 17,
  Bar(t) 84 HP @ wave 21).
- Enemies seen: Slime, Bat, Goblin, Goblin King (boss).
- Drops: coins (+16..+25 each), gems (+5ish; 0 -> 20 by wave 17).
- Boss drop: an EGG ("Mountain Egg Lvl.3") added to hotbar -> place in nest.
  Egg in nest shows "E Skip" prompt (pay to skip hatch timer).
- Badge awarded for beating first boss.
- Losing all dragons ends run; best wave saved; restart from wave 1 (or checkpoint?).

## Run end
- "Fight End!" panel: Wave reached (24), Coins Earned (4.5K), Mobs Killed (23).
  Buttons: "Revive & Continue" (likely Robux/dev product - not pressed), "No Thanks".
- Gems also awarded at run end (+100 popups).

  -> plot evolution gated by breed count + coins.
- Extra NEST slots ring the plot as white fluffy nests with a price tag (1.5K each seen; 3 owned at start).
- "Dragon's Power — Roll some Powers!" feature locked until a wave milestone (code FreeSpin = free roll).
- Hub: "Harvest Hunt" limited event (ends in 12d), "Elemental Bundle" R$199,
  LIMITED dragon "Dreadhorn" stand with roll odds shown (40% / 39% / 15% / 5% ...).
