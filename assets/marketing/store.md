# Merge Sea Monsters: store page

## Name
Merge Sea Monsters 🌊 Hatch & Fuse

## Description (paste as is, under 1000 characters)
🎁 Codes: SEAMONSTER, AHOY, FREESPIN

🥚 HOW TO PLAY
Pull the winch on your pirate ship to haul up monster eggs, hatch them in your nests, then send your sea dragons out to battle on the open water!

❤️ FUSE
Pick two monsters of the same level and fuse them into a stronger one. Mix Tide, Coral, Storm and Abyss to discover hybrids!

⭐ 34 SEA MONSTERS
Collect Common to Mythic monsters. Roll Golden and Rainbow mutations for huge stats!

⚔️ DIVE DEEPER
Beat mini bosses and giant bosses every 15 depths. Reach DEPTH 240 to defeat Maw, the Abyss King, and rescue your monster!

📖 SPELLS
Win battles to find spell cards. Cast them at the Spellbook for more luck, more eggs and more damage.

🏝️ HARBOUR
Visit the Egg Shop, the Abyss Crate and the Tide Festival in the harbour town.

💤 Your team keeps battling while you are offline!

👍 Like and Favorite for more updates!
